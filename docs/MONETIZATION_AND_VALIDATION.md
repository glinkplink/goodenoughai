# Monetization and validation

Revenue projections are **hypotheses**, not guarantees. Validate demand before investing in payment infrastructure.

## Pre-launch discovery and pilot investment gate

Begin problem and positioning interviews during Phases 2–4; this is research, not a paid-offer tier and not permission to publish unverified benchmark claims. Recruit **8–12** automation builders from the target audience and test the problem, current decision process, alternatives used, and interest in model-selection evidence.

After the Phase 4 pilot passes its technical quality gates, privately show a clearly labeled sample pilot report. Pilot results remain diagnostic and must not be published as stable rankings. Before Phase 5, record a continue/pivot/stop decision using:

- At least **3** participants saying the evidence would change or confirm a model decision, or volunteering a real workflow for follow-up
- At least **1** credible willingness-to-pay signal for a custom comparison
- Evidence that users recognize a benefit beyond Checkstack-style extraction comparison, generic model leaderboards, or live routing alone

If the gate misses, evaluate a custom-report or self-service-evaluation wedge, revise positioning, or stop before funding the full stable batch and static site. A pivot requires its own scope and decision update; it is not silently added to the MVP.

## Validation sequence

Execute in order — do not skip to later tiers without evidence:

| Phase | Offer | Purpose |
|-------|-------|---------|
| 1 | **Public useful benchmark** | Trust, SEO, methodology credibility |
| 2 | **Custom benchmark reports** | First revenue; proves willingness to pay |
| 3 | **Affiliate/referral** (ethical disclosure) | Supplementary; never affects rankings |
| 4 | **Pro filtering and exports** | Recurring revenue from power users |
| 5 | **Private benchmark projects** | Higher-touch customer workflows |
| 6 | **Benchmark API** | Integration into routing tools |
| 7 | **Sponsored evaluations** | Provider-paid testing; methodology unchanged |
| 8 | **Ads** | Only after legitimate recurring traffic |

## Initial paid offer

**Custom benchmark report** (fastest path to first dollar):

> Submit representative examples from your workflow. Receive a private model comparison showing the cheapest reliable model and recommended routing strategy.

### Candidate pricing hypotheses

| Package | Models | Examples | Price (hypothesis) | Deliverables |
|---------|--------|----------|-------------------|--------------|
| Quick comparison | 3 | Up to 25 | $49 | Basic recommendation |
| Full comparison | 6–10 | Up to 100 | $149 | Failure analysis, cost projection, routing |
| Enterprise workflow | 10+ | 100+ | $299–$999 | Custom suite, escalation sim, consultation |

From `roadmap.md`: one $149 report every two weeks ≈ $10/day averaged — **ASSUMPTION**, not forecast.

### What a custom benchmark includes

- Private intake of customer workflow samples (NDA-friendly handling)
- Selection of 3–10 inexpensive models matching task type
- Deterministic scoring on customer schema where possible
- Failure analysis with representative raw outputs
- Cost per accepted result projection at stated volume
- Escalation routing recommendation
- Written summary (not autogenerated fluff)

### Required customer inputs

- Task type (extraction / classification / normalization)
- 10–100 representative input examples
- Desired output schema or categories
- Volume estimate (jobs/month)
- Latency tolerance
- Local vs cloud constraint
- Sensitive data flag ( affects model selection )

### Minimum viable delivery workflow

1. Hosted intake form on the static site (no payment or local data persistence)
2. Manual review of feasibility
3. Author private cases from sanitized examples
4. Run benchmark batch on agreed models
5. Human-written report with scores and recommendation
6. Invoice manually (Stripe deferred)

### Intake and response policy

The hosted qualification form collects:

- Contact name and email
- Task family and short workflow description
- Number of representative examples available (do not upload sensitive examples at this stage)
- Desired schema, labels, or categories
- Estimated monthly volume and latency tolerance
- Local/cloud preference and structured-output requirement
- Sensitive/private data flag and relevant provider constraints

Respond within **three business days** with fit, required sanitization/data handling, proposed scope, price, and delivery estimate. Request actual sensitive examples only after manual qualification. Unless a written engagement specifies otherwise, delete private inputs, raw outputs, and operational copies within 30 calendar days after final delivery or earlier withdrawal and record completion. Package prices and delivery estimates remain engagement-specific hypotheses until validated.

## Evidence to continue

- Users engage with task-specific pages, not just homepage
- Custom benchmark inquiries reference real workflows
- At least one paid custom report completed
- Benchmark results stable enough to recommend confidently
- Differentiation from Checkstack, generic leaderboards, and live routers acknowledged by users

## Evidence to pivot

- Users only want generic "best LLM" rankings
- Nobody submits real workflows
- Results too prompt-sensitive to trust
- Cloud pricing/version churn makes maintenance unmanageable
- Corpus doesn't distinguish models meaningfully
- Users prefer raw test tools over published database
- Zero payment interest after targeted outreach

## Kill criteria

Stop or major pivot if after validation period:

- No paid custom benchmark and no pipeline
- < 50 qualified visitors with outreach effort
- Maintainer cannot keep suite current due to provider churn
- Scoring trust broken by discovered defects

## Conflict-of-interest policy

- Sponsorship **cannot** alter scoring, rankings, or methodology
- Sponsored evaluations disclosed: *"Sponsored evaluation requested by Provider X. Scoring unchanged."*
- Affiliate links labeled; recommendations driven by benchmark data
- No pay-for-rank

## Affiliate opportunities

Model hosts and API routers may offer referral programs. Link only to recommended providers when benchmark supports recommendation. Independent ranking always.
